History & Passion

The collection of koopmansfineart.com originated from a deep-rooted fascination with classical European painting. What began as a personal journey of discovery through the refined masterpieces of the nineteenth century has grown into a carefully curated private collection. The collection focuses particularly on atmospheric still lifes, interiors, and landscapes that bear witness to exceptional craftsmanship and a striking historical character.

We believe that every antique painting is a unique window to the past that must be treated with respect. For this reason, we dedicate a great deal of time to historical provenance research, material analysis, and the careful conservation of both the canvases and their monumental, hand-gilded frames.

About Me

My name is Merijn Koopmans.

The most beautiful aspect of this passion is the journey of discovery: intensively searching regional auction houses, estate sales, and private collections for that one hidden gem. A yellowed, darkened layer of varnish or a subtle, almost illegible signature often conceals the hand of a classical master—such as the highly refined still lifes of Arnoud Wydeveld or the atmospheric maritime views of Hendrik Hulk.

Conserving and researching these works is, for me, a form of historical respect. Through koopmansfineart.com, I hope to share this passion and the tangible beauty of classical craftsmanship with fellow collectors and enthusiasts.
